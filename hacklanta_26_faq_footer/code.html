HACKLANTA '26 PROTOCOL ONLINE
Got an unlisted anomaly? Reach directly via #ask-organizers on Discord.

Any currently enrolled undergraduate, graduate, or high school student (18+) is eligible to apply. We welcome builders from all skill levels and backgrounds!

Hacklanta is 100% free for all accepted participants! Meals, snacks, drinks, swag, and access to all sponsor workshops are provided throughout the 36 hours.

Teams can consist of up to 4 hackers. You are also welcome to hack solo, though we strongly encourage teaming up to share skills and energy.

Not at all! We host dedicated team formation sessions, icebreakers, and a Team Finder portal before and during kickoff so you can find your crew.

Hacklanta '26 will be hosted in Atlanta, GA at a vibrant venue in the heart of Midtown. Detailed check-in logistics and venue maps are sent upon acceptance.

Hacklanta '26 is an in-person festival experience. All judging, ceremonies, mentor desks, and sponsor booths take place live on the floor.

Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis. Once approved, you'll receive an invitation email with instructions to RSVP and join our Discord community.
