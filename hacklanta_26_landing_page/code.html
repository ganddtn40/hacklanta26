What is
Hacklanta?

Hacklanta's back, and this one's bigger than the first. 36 hours straight, festival energy the whole way through: no breaks, no sleep, just building whatever makes you tick with 1,000+ collegiate creators from across North America.

This time, sponsors are on the floor running a live sponsor fair with custom challenges, dedicated dev mentors, and fast-track hiring funnels. Hardware labs, zero-latency cloud credits, midnight food truck drops, and electric DJ sets. Seats are strictly limited, so apply now.

FAQ

Got questions before dropping into Atlanta? Here is everything you need to know about eligibility, travel grants, teaming up, and what to pack.
Any currently enrolled undergraduate, graduate, or high school student (18+) from any accredited institution can apply. All majors are warmly welcomed—developers, hardware hackers, UI/UX designers, and domain enthusiasts alike.
Hacklanta is 100% free for all accepted participants! We supply all your meals, snacks, caffeinated beverages, hardware lab equipment, and legendary festival swag over the entire 36-hour sprint.
Teams can have between 1 and 4 members. While you can compete solo, building in teams of 3–4 is highly encouraged to maximize what you can ship over 36 hours.
Not at all! We host structured team-formation workshops on Discord before the hackathon and live speed-matching mixers at opening ceremonies on Friday night.
Hacklanta '26 takes place in the heart of Atlanta’s Tech Square district. Detailed venue access passes, parking validation, and transit directions will be sent directly with your acceptance packet.
Hacklanta '26 is exclusively an in-person festival experience. All judging, ceremonies, stage showcases, and sponsor workshops require on-site participation to maintain our high-voltage festival atmosphere.
Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis. Once accepted, you will receive an invitation to RSVP and gain instant access to our Discord community, Team Finder portal, and hardware reservation queues.
